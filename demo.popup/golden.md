The timed-activity popup screen (限时活动) raised by the reward page's "模拟活动弹窗" button: a
white centred card over the dimmed reward page, holding the title "限时活动", the line
"这是挡路弹窗，点关闭后再领奖。" and a green "关闭" button. The dimmed page behind it shows the
reward page's title but its other anchors rule themselves out while this card is up. The 关闭
caption's own ink is what this anchor recognises, and tapping it returns to the reward page.
