Desktop launcher of the test device (the device is in landscape here). The wallpaper with
the star field and the dock along the bottom is fixed; the row of app icons stays put too.
The CampCat Idle Demo icon -- a green rounded square holding a white cat face with an
orange chimney -- is the control this anchor recognises, and tapping it starts the game.
Nothing about the clock, the battery or the wallpaper's light streaks is reliable.
